## Brake Caliper Savings Strategy

- **Core Thesis**: Drive a 15%+ caliper cost reduction by sequencing high-ROI contract levers with synchronized engineering, supply chain, and commercial actions while hardwiring flexibility for geopolitical and market volatility.
- **Value Model**: Blend should-cost baseline, lane-by-lane logistics model, and sensitivity analysis on iron, aluminum, energy, labor, and FX to prioritize levers delivering fastest payback and resilience.

## Actionable Approach

- **Joint Productivity Roadmap (Highest ROI)**: Codify VA/VE milestones, automation upgrades, packaging changes, and lean deployments into the contract; ensure savings commitments trigger price changes automatically and share upside once Ford’s price floor is met.
- **Capital Co-investment Clauses**: Pre-negotiate Ford support for casting automation, in-line inspection, and high-density tooling; tie reimbursements to OEE, scrap, and throughput targets, enabling step-change cost-down with controlled risk.
- **Open-Book Cost Baselines**: Establish transparent cost stack with quarterly reconciliations using third-party verified indices; enables rapid price resets and supports collaboration on new levers.
- **Raw-Material Escalators/De-escalators**: Link prices to published indices with guardrails; stabilizes margins under commodity swings and speeds pass-through when markets ease.
- **Performance Rebates & Penalties**: Align 1–2% of contract value with OTIF, PPM, and premium freight to reinforce execution discipline and self-fund continuous improvement.
- **Volume & Mix Protection**: Structure tiered pricing and make-load compensation for engineering-driven complexity; preserves ROI on capital and discourages volatility-induced penalties.
- **Structured Renegotiation Gates**: Set semiannual review triggers tied to macro indicators (tariffs, FX thresholds) and technology readiness to keep terms aligned without reneging.
- **Digital Transparency (ROI Enabler)**: Mandate EDI, shared dashboards, and predictive analytics for demand, cost, and logistics to maintain trust and shorten dispute cycles.

## Detailed Work Plan

- **Phase 0 (Weeks 0–4)**: Build should-cost and risk models; map supplier footprint, capacity, and geopolitical exposure; baseline quality validation timelines and technology maturity by supplier.
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 4–10)**: Run contract workshops with Ford purchasing, engineering, finance, and top suppliers; quantify ROI per lever; draft roadmap, capital, and cost transparency clauses; secure legal review.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 10–18)**: Pilot roadmap + capital clauses with one high-volume caliper family; deploy automation or process improvements; launch digital cost tracker; validate engineering tests to avoid line stoppages.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 18–32)**: Scale to remaining suppliers; activate indexed pricing, performance rebates, and volume protections; embed structured renegotiation cadence; integrate logistics changes (regional finishing, milk-runs, reusable packaging).
- **Phase 4 (Weeks 32+)**: Continuous improvement loop; quarterly cost stack reconciliation; adjust roadmap for new tech readiness, product refreshes, or regulatory changes; feed results into Ford’s SIOP.

## Global Complexity Integration

- **Geopolitical Shifts**: Maintain dual-source regions (NA, EU, APAC) with contract clauses for rapid volume reallocation; include tariff/sanction triggers in renegotiation gates.
- **Economic Volatility**: Use indexed pricing and open-book reconciliations to stabilize cash flow; keep contingency scenarios for FX swings and energy surcharges.
- **Engineering Tests**: Link roadmap milestones to validation gates; prebook lab time and align PPAP sequencing to avoid launch delays.
- **Technology Readiness**: Stage capital co-investments only after TRL assessment; include fallback processes if automation ROI slips.
- **Market Fluctuations**: Embed demand flex bands and VMI/kanban triggers in contracts; ensure volume protections preserve supplier viability during demand shocks.

## Counterpoints & Mitigations

- **ROI Doubt on Automation**: Mitigate with shared payback models, phased capex release, and performance-based reimbursement.
- **Supplier Confidentiality Concerns**: Use third-party audits and NDAs for open-book data; provide reciprocal transparency on Ford forecasts.
- **Index Volatility Overshoot**: Apply collar bands and look-back averaging; add renegotiation clause if indices move beyond preset thresholds.
- **Engineering Change Fatigue**: Coordinate VA/VE cadence with Ford product teams; bundle changes to minimize repeated validation.
- **Operational KPI Penalties**: Ensure baseline metrics are jointly agreed; include cure periods and support resources before penalties activate.
- **Digital Integration Resistance**: Offer co-funded EDI/onboarding support; establish governance that rewards data accuracy.
- **Geopolitical Production Risk**: Maintain buffer inventory at regional hubs; secure alternate transport lanes and insurance riders.
